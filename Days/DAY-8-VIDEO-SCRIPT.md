## Why this one works

- **Stakes in frame one** — "you almost lost it all" is a real fear for players, so they watch to see the fix.
- **Proof on screen** — showing the coins survive a rejoin (and the Output log) makes it believable, not just talk.
- **The unfinished part is the hook, not a weakness** — "it's not done, I finish tomorrow" is a built-in reason to come back. You did this on Day 1 and it worked.
- **One specific ask** — "what color should the rarest trail be?" is easy to answer, so comments pour in (top 2026 ranking signal).

## Editing (CapCut)

- Auto-caption, bump the font, keep it centered and bold.
- **Cut every 1.5–2s.** The Output-log shot should be a quick 1s flash, not lingering.
- Put a **zoom-punch** on the Collection UI when it opens (8–16s) — that's the satisfying moment.
- No intro. Motion in frame one. Loop the end into the start.
- Keep it **38–42s**.
