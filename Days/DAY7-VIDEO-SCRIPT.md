# Day 7 — YouTube Shorts Script (Viral Build)

**Length:** 38–42s · **Voiceover:** your own voice, English (original audio = algorithm boost under 50k) · **Captions:** big, bold, always on (most viewers watch muted)

**What makes this one go further than your other videos:**
1. It opens on the *result* (a Legendary drop), not "today I…" — no slow intro to swipe past.
2. It uses a **curiosity gap** in the first line so people wait for the answer.
3. It puts a **real viewer's comment on screen** — proof that commenting gets you in the game, which is what drives *your* comments up (comment depth is a top 2026 ranking signal).
4. It ends on an **open question + loop**, so the video replays and the comments fill up.

---

## Scene-by-scene script

| Time | 🎬 SCENE (what's on screen) | 🗣️ Voiceover | 🔤 On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0–2s** — HOOK | Extreme close-up: an egg cracks open and a **gold "LEGENDARY" popup** bursts out. Freeze-punch on it. | "A random comment changed my whole game." | **A VIEWER CHANGED MY GAME 🐧** |
| **2–5s** — SETUP | Screen recording: player finishes an obby, coins tick up, walks to the egg in the lobby. | "You grind coins in the obbies…" | *earn coins → buy eggs* |
| **5–11s** — PAYOFF | Hold E on the egg, coins drop 100 → 0, egg shakes and cracks, reward popup appears (show a Rare/Epic). | "…and spend them on eggs for random penguin skins and trails." | *Common → Legendary* |
| **11–17s** — CREDIT | Screenshot of the **actual viewer comment** ("maybe different eggs…"), zoom-punch + circle it. | "This was a comment from day one. I told you — your ideas go straight in." | **YOUR COMMENT = IN THE GAME** |
| **17–25s** — TENSION | Fast montage of Obby #2: two clean jumps, then **one fail clip** (character falls), then the finish pad. | "I also finished obby number two… and honestly it's brutal." | *Obby #2 — can you beat it?* |
| **25–33s** — THE ASK | Back on the egg / a list of possible rewards. | "But I need you: what should the *rarest* egg drop be? Best idea is in the game tomorrow." | **WHAT'S THE RAREST EGG? 👇** |
| **33–40s** — LOOP | Hard-cut back to the exact gold Legendary popup from the hook. | "Day 7 of building games until I'm a Top 100 Roblox developer." | *Day 7 → Top 100 Roblox dev* |

## Hook A/B test (change ONLY the first 2 seconds)

1. **"A random comment changed my whole game."** ← primary (curiosity gap)
2. **"This viewer has been here since day one — so I put their idea in the game."**
3. **"I let my comments design my game… and it actually worked."**

Post the same edit, swap only line 1. Whichever hook holds the higher average-view %, that's your formula for the next 10 Shorts.

---

## Virality levers baked in (keep these)

- **Result first.** Frame one is the gold popup — the best moment, not the last moment.
- **Curiosity gap.** "A comment changed my game" makes people wait to find out *how*.
- **Proof of reward.** Showing a real viewer's idea in-game makes strangers comment, because commenting visibly pays off.
- **Tension beat.** The single fail clip in Obby #2 spikes attention right when retention usually dips.
- **One clear question.** Not "what do you think?" — a specific, answerable ask ("rarest egg?").
- **Seamless loop.** Ending = beginning, so rewatches rack up watch time (the #1 metric now).

## Editing (CapCut)

- Auto-caption, then **bump font size** and center it — bold and readable on mobile.
- **Cut every 1.5–2s.** No dead air.
- **Zoom-punch** on the comment (11–17s) so people actually read it.
- No intro, no logo, no "hey guys." Motion in frame one.
- Keep it **38–42s** — don't pad.

## After you post

- **Pin:** "What should the rarest egg drop be? 👇 Best idea is in tomorrow's video."
- Reply to every comment in the first hour, each ending in a question.
- Watch **average view %** — the moment it clears 50%, reuse this exact structure.
